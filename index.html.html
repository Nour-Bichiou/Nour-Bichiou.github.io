About

A little about me

Tunis, Tunisia

I'm a fifth-year student in Industrial Computing and Automation Engineering at INSAT, focusing on AI. Over the last two years most of what I've built has involved language models in some way: retrieval pipelines, agent workflows, and the scoring and checks around them.

What I enjoy most is the part where a model has to fit into something bigger. At Talan, that meant helping build a copilot that compares data before and after a database migration and explains why records don't match. At ThanksBoss, it meant a Django backend and a scoring system for an interview-simulation app with real users.

I also like building things end to end on my own time, from a voice tutor for Tunisian Derja to a flight-price model on Databricks. Right now I'm looking for a 4 to 6 month end-of-studies internship in AI/ML or data science, starting February 2027.
